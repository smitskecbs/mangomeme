About ManGo

Sometimes a simple thing becomes more than what it is.

FAQ

How do I qualify?
There are no automatic rewards. The whitelist is based on genuine participation, positive contributions and long-term support for the community.
When is the airdrop?
There is no fixed date. Liquidity will only be added when the community has grown enough. After that, eligible early supporters may receive a community airdrop.
